# Interface direction

The brief supplies two product references. [Dovetail's timestamped transcript summaries](https://dovetail.com/changelog/enhanced-transcription-summaries/) motivate a video, passage and source-link workspace. [W&B Reports](https://wandb.ai/site/reports/) motivates a separate view for comparisons, dataset coverage and limitations. Refero research was attempted on 2026-11-01, but its connected account returned `NO_SUBSCRIPTION`; the supplied references and bundled design craft guidance are the design basis.

Reference lock: compact research tool, white panels on a cool gray canvas, narrow left rail, crisp sans-serif hierarchy, hairline borders, and teal reserved for navigation and actions. Amber labels mark hypotheses or unavailable evidence. No decorative brain graphic appears in lesson analysis; the Evidence view reserves an explicitly predicted-only slot. The source timestamp is the memorable interaction: it connects the transcript, action trace and finding to the video.

The workflow is Projects → Lesson → Simulation → Findings → Evidence. A user can return to an earlier version from its child. Every view keeps the evidence status readable without relying on color alone.
